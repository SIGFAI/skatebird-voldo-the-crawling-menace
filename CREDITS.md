# Credits: Voldo the Crawling Menace (SkateBIRD mod)

All art and sound in this mod were generated for it with fal (original, no third-party assets):

- skin.png, cloth.png: z-image turbo tiling textures (fal-ai/z-image/turbo/tiling), cropped and contrast-adjusted.
- coin_icon.png, coin_copper.png, coin_silver.png: nano-banana-2 pixel-art coin, background removed with BiRefNet v2; copper and silver are hue-shifted copies.
- growl.wav, screech.wav, hit.wav, coin.wav, death.wav, stun.wav: ElevenLabs sound-effects v2 via fal, converted to 16-bit PCM WAV.

Voldo's 3D body, animation, AI, boss bar and coin counter are original code (built from Unity primitives).
Inspired by Terraria (boss health bar, coins, damage numbers); no Terraria assets are used.
